Understand the condition

SMA Type 1 & Type 2

What is SMA?

Spinal Muscular Atrophy (SMA) is a genetic condition that affects motor neurons, leading to progressive muscle weakness. Severity and needs vary from child to child.

This website is for support and fundraising, not medical diagnosis or treatment advice.

SMA Type 1

  • Usually begins in infancy.
  • Can cause significant muscle weakness and difficulties with feeding or breathing.
  • Children may need complex, ongoing multidisciplinary care.

SMA Type 2

  • Often begins later in infancy or early childhood.
  • Weakness can affect sitting, movement and daily activities.
  • Respiratory, orthopedic and rehabilitation support may be needed.

Treatment & support

Families may face substantial costs for prescribed disease-modifying treatment, hospital care, respiratory equipment, nutrition, physiotherapy and other supportive services.

Treatment decisions must come from the child's medical team.
