Understanding SMA Type 1 & 2

What is SMA?

Spinal Muscular Atrophy is a genetic condition affecting motor neurons and causing muscle weakness. Severity and care needs vary between children.

This information is educational and does not replace a child's treating medical team.

SMA Type 1

  • Usually begins in infancy.
  • May involve significant weakness and breathing or feeding difficulties.
  • Often requires complex multidisciplinary care.

SMA Type 2

  • Often begins later in infancy or early childhood.
  • Weakness can affect sitting, movement and daily activities.
  • Respiratory, orthopedic and rehabilitation support may be needed.

Treatment & family costs

Families may face costs for prescribed disease-modifying treatment, hospital care, respiratory equipment, nutrition, physiotherapy and other supportive services.
